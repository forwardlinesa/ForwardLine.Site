FAQ

Questions before you book?

Here are short, straight answers.

What does ForwardLine do?

We turn ad clicks into booked meetings for South African IT companies and MSPs. We build the page, sales video and form. Our AI caller Susie phones each lead within 5 minutes.

Who is it for?

IT companies and MSPs in South Africa that spend R10,000 or more a month on ads. Someone on your team takes the meetings.

My ads get clicks but no new clients. Can you help?

Often the ad is fine and the leak comes after the click. We fix the page, the video, the form and the call. Your agency keeps the ads.

What is a VSL?

VSL means video sales letter. It’s a short sales video on your page. It answers the questions a first sales call usually covers.

Do you write my sales video script?

You answer our questions in order. You send the answers as a voice note, text or a call. We review them and help you fix the script. You get this after signing.

Who is Susie?

Susie is our AI caller. She phones each lead within 5 minutes, says she’s AI, asks a few questions and books the meeting in your calendar.

How do you turn cold leads into warm leads?

Cold leads don’t know you yet. The page and video show them who you are. The form shows who’s serious. Then Susie phones them.

Do you build done-for-you sales funnels?

Yes. We build a VSL funnel: page, sales video, form and call. You take the meetings.

Is this conversion rate optimisation?

Yes. CRO and pipeline automation are the trade names. We keep testing your page and we automate the first call.

Do I keep my agency?

Yes. They run the ads. We take over after the click.

What does “if you qualify” mean?

Your page turns under about 3% of visitors into leads today, and you run at least R10,000 a month in ads. We check this on the call. We agree your starting number before we begin.

What does it cost?

Our first MSP partners pay R9,375 a month excl VAT with no setup fee, on a 12-month term. It’s below our cost. We do it for the proof. After that it’s R12,500 a month plus a once-off setup fee, excl VAT. Your ad spend stays separate.

What do you need from me?

One DNS record, which takes about 5 minutes. We help on a screen share. Your agency points the ads to the new page.
